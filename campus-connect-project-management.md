# Campus Connect / Ask Winnie — Project Management

## Project Management Approach

The Campus Connect / Ask Winnie project considered the major project management activities required to plan and organise the proposed IT solution.

The project documentation covered:

* Scope management
* Schedule management
* Resource management
* Risk management
* Communication management
* Stakeholder management
* Responsibility allocation
* Project monitoring

---

## Scope Management

Scope management was used to define what the proposed Campus Connect solution would include and exclude.

The project identified specific features that were in scope, including the Ask Winnie chatbot, personalised study planning, timetable and assessment information, student alerts, FAQs, wellbeing support, administrative support, and text-based interaction.

The project also defined features that were outside the proposed scope, such as course enrolment changes, grades and academic records, financial transactions, professional health services, sensitive medical records, modification of university records, voice and multilingual support, and replacement of university staff.

Clearly defining the scope was intended to control project changes, prevent unnecessary work, and support appropriate use of project resources.

---

## Schedule Management

The project used schedule management to organise activities, task durations, dependencies, and project sequencing.

The planned activities included:

* Smart Study Schedule
* Smart Notifications
* Wellbeing and Support
* Smart FAQs
* Administration

The project documentation included a network diagram and critical path analysis to demonstrate task dependencies and project sequencing.

The identified critical path was:

**Start → Activity 3 → Activity 4 → Activity 5 → Finish**

This analysis helped demonstrate how project activities could be sequenced and monitored.

---

## Resource Management

The project considered human, technical, and project resources required to develop the proposed solution.

The proposed team structure included:

* Project Manager
* Business Analyst
* AI/Chatbot Developer
* UI/UX Designer
* Content/Knowledge Specialist
* Tester / QA

Responsibilities were allocated according to each role.

For example, the Business Analyst role included gathering stakeholder requirements, analysing support processes, and documenting system requirements, while the Tester / QA role focused on testing chatbot responses, integration, usability, and accuracy.

The resource allocation strategy also considered team capacity, parallel work, progress monitoring, and supporting team members when unexpected workload or delays occurred.

---

## Risk Management

The project identified several potential risks, including:

* Unequal distribution of resources
* Team member availability
* Delays in chatbot development or Moodle integration
* Missing or invalid information
* Technical integration problems
* Limited team experience with AI chatbots

The project proposed mitigation strategies such as:

* Allocating work according to team skills and capacity
* Providing knowledge sharing and cross-support
* Monitoring project progress
* Providing coaching when required
* Testing the chatbot throughout development
* Communicating regularly with key stakeholders
* Focusing resources on critical activities

## RACI Matrix

A RACI matrix was used to clarify responsibilities during different project activities.

RACI represents:

* **Responsible** — the person completing the task
* **Accountable** — the person accountable for the result
* **Consulted** — people providing advice or feedback
* **Informed** — people receiving project updates

The project applied the RACI approach to activities including:

* Project planning
* Requirements gathering
* Interface design
* System development
* System testing
* Issue correction
* Final review

## Key Project Management Learning

This project helped develop an understanding of how different project management areas work together.

Effective project planning requires consideration of:

**Scope → Schedule → Resources → Risks → Stakeholders → Communication → Monitoring**

Defining the scope helps control project boundaries. Scheduling helps organise dependencies and activities. Resource management supports appropriate allocation of people and tools. Risk management helps identify potential problems early. Stakeholder and communication management support collaboration and feedback.

Together, these areas contribute to more organised and controlled IT project delivery.

# Campus Connect / Ask Winnie

## Project Overview

Campus Connect / Ask Winnie is a proposed student support solution designed as an additional feature within the university's Moodle environment.

The project was developed as part of a university group project in IT Project Management. The idea was based on challenges experienced by students when accessing information and support through different channels.

The proposed solution combines student support features with an AI-powered chatbot called **Ask Winnie** to make general information and support easier to access in one place.

## Project Problem

Students currently access information and support through different channels, including the university website, Moodle, email, SMS, and in-person support.

The project identified that finding answers to general questions could be time-consuming and that students may need to rely on staff for repetitive enquiries.

The proposed solution aimed to provide a more convenient digital support channel while continuing to direct students to appropriate staff and existing support services when required.

## Proposed Solution

Ask Winnie was proposed as an AI chatbot integrated into the university's Moodle environment.

The chatbot would provide students with quick access to general information and frequently asked questions relating to areas such as:

* Enrolment information
* Courses
* Fees
* Timetables
* Campus facilities
* Student support

The proposed chatbot would not replace existing staff support. More complex or specific enquiries would be redirected to the appropriate university staff member or existing support channel.

## Project Objectives

The project aimed to:

* Help students organise their study time
* Track assignment deadlines and important dates
* Provide useful notifications
* Provide a platform for questions and guidance
* Make wellbeing information and support easier to access
* Direct students to appropriate support when needed

## Proposed Features

The Campus Connect solution included:

1. **Smart Study Schedule**
2. **Smart Notifications**
3. **Wellbeing and Support**
4. **Smart FAQs**
5. **Administrative Support**
6. **Ask Winnie AI Chatbot**
7. **Text-based student interaction**

These features were designed to work alongside existing student support services.

## Project Scope

The project defined both in-scope and out-of-scope functionality.

### In Scope

* Ask Winnie chatbot
* Personalised study planning
* Timetable and assessment information
* Student alerts and reminders
* Student FAQs
* Wellbeing and support guidance
* Administrative support
* Text-based interaction

### Out of Scope

The proposed solution would not:

* Change course enrolments
* Provide grades or detailed academic records
* Process payments or financial transactions
* Provide medical diagnosis or professional counselling
* Access sensitive medical records
* Modify official university records
* Provide voice or multilingual interaction initially
* Replace university staff or make official decisions

The scope boundaries were intended to prevent unnecessary project expansion and support effective allocation of project time and resources.
